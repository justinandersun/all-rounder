The All-Rounder

A General Physical Fitness Test

Why It Matters

Modern life is comfortable, and comfort has a cost. Cars, chairs, and screens have removed the daily physical demands that once kept men capable. Left unchecked, this comfort produces soft, weak, and fragile bodies. This test sets out a fixed, proven standard of general physical fitness: strength, endurance, mobility, and work capacity, measured against benchmarks a man can actually train toward. Physical fitness is the foundation on which the rest of life is built, and it is worth deliberate investment. Every event here can be trained and tested with a barbell, a stopwatch, and open ground.

How It Works

The test is designed to be completed in one day. It spans 15 events and offers 100 total points.

Every event is scored against a fixed benchmark drawn from your height or bodyweight. Four movement checks are pass/fail and worth one point each. The remaining events award partial credit. Meeting or exceeding the benchmark earns full points, and any shortfall costs points. Scoring at least one point on all 15 events earns one additional point. Your total score determines your grade:

Methodology

The All-Rounder is a practical standard for general male fitness. Its benchmarks draw from established strength standards, field fitness tests, coaching benchmarks for physical capability, and historical all-around fitness standards.[1–5] Functional movement tests are also informed by research associating balance and the ability to rise from the floor with health and functional outcomes later in life.[6–7]

The standards are intentionally broad rather than specialized. They are designed to represent a solid intermediate level of strength, conditioning, muscular endurance, power, movement, grip, carrying capacity, running, and swimming. Meeting every benchmark should require regular training across multiple physical domains without requiring elite performance in any one of them.

The All-Rounder is a standard, not a population norm. A population norm describes how a person compares with peers; a standard defines a level of capability worth achieving and maintaining. The benchmarks therefore do not adjust downward with age. The goal is to measure the physical capability you possess, not your fitness relative to others your age.

Disclaimer

The All-Rounder is an independently developed fitness challenge and has not been clinically validated as a complete fitness assessment. Its benchmarks should not be interpreted as medical standards, diagnoses, or guarantees of health, longevity, or ability to perform any particular occupation or emergency task.

Exercise and fitness testing involve risk, particularly maximal or near-maximal lifting, running, and swimming. Participants are responsible for exercising within their abilities, using appropriate technique and safety equipment, and seeking qualified medical or professional guidance when appropriate.
